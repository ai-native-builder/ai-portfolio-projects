# Humble Group — AI & Automation Lead

**Brands:** Humble Grape (multi-site wine bar) + Vivat Bacchus (South African cuisine & wine)  
**Location:** London (hybrid — across sites and remote)  
**Salary:** £35,000–£45,000 depending on experience  
**Start date:** June/July 2025  
**Reports to:** CEO directly  
**Original URL:** https://humblegrape.teamtailor.com/jobs/7622691-ai-automation-lead

---

## The Role

Newly created position. Work directly with the CEO and senior leadership to identify, build, and deploy AI-powered solutions across the business — one project at a time, every week. Internal AI product builder with commercial instincts.

No slide decks. No discovery phases. First working solution shipped within two weeks of starting. After that, one new solution delivered every week.

---

## First Projects

- **Hyper-personalised outbound outreach agent** — scraping Google Maps, enriching leads, verifying emails, crafting bespoke messages for every recipient
- Marketing automation using Claude, Notion, and LLM tools — raw inputs → polished LinkedIn posts, emails, social content
- Vision-based stock-take app — photograph wine shelves, auto-populate inventory
- AI-powered HR chatbot trained on the employee handbook
- API integrations connecting SevenRooms, MarketMan, Lightspeed POS into management dashboards

---

## Must-Haves

- You've already built something — a Claude-powered tool, a GPT, an API integration, a chatbot — and can show what it does
- Comfortable using AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT) to build and iterate without being a traditional developer
- Problem-solver first, tool-agnostic second
- Can communicate clearly with non-technical stakeholders

## Strong Advantages

- Experience with n8n, Make, Zapier or similar workflow automation tools
- Familiarity with prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, or LLM APIs
- Any exposure to hospitality tech stacks (POS, reservations, inventory, HR)
- Portfolio of shipped projects

## What Success Looks Like

Assessed on delivery cadence, impact, and initiative. Perform and the role grows fast in scope, seniority, and salary.
