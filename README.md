# AI Native Builder Projects

Real briefs. Real problems. Build them to get hired.

---

## Why This Exists

Founders want builders who ship with AI. Job descriptions still ask for CS degrees and five years of experience. HR writes the JD. The founder does the interview. They're looking for completely different things.

[AI Native Builder](https://www.ai-native-builder.com/) is a job board that cuts through that noise — real roles for people who build and ship with AI.

This repo is the practice ground. Every brief came from a real company. Work through them, post your results, and learn from what others built.

---

## How It Works

1. Read `PRINCIPLES.md` first — the framework you'll use to scope every project
2. Pick a project by category
3. Do discovery before touching code
4. Keep a build log as you go — not after
5. Self-score against the criteria in the brief
6. Post in [r/AINativeBuilder](https://www.reddit.com/r/AINativeBuilder/) with the **Feedback Request** flair

## The Rules

- No hints. Discovery is part of the project.
- Keep your build log as you go.
- Track your time honestly.
- Self-score before asking for feedback.

---

## Feedback and Community

Post your project in [r/AINativeBuilder](https://www.reddit.com/r/AINativeBuilder/) using the **Feedback Request** flair. Include your scope document, build log, self-score, and a link to your code.

For now I'll review and comment on every submission. The goal is to build an open community where builders learn from each other's work — the more people post, the better the discussion gets.

**What to include in your post:**
- Scope document — what you built, what you cut, and why
- Build log with time tracking
- Self-score on the five dimensions from the brief
- Link to your code
